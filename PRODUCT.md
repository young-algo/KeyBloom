# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KeyBloom has two audiences. A baby or toddler uses the full-screen play surface by pressing keys, clicking, scrolling, and dragging without needing to understand an interface. A parent or caregiver uses the setup window to choose a visual palette, motion level, labels, and input-lock strength before handing over the Mac.

## Product Purpose

KeyBloom turns ordinary keyboard and pointer input into an immediate, colorful, nonverbal reward while keeping the computer protected from accidental input. Success means the child can discover a stable connection between an action and a visual response, while the adult can start and exit the experience confidently without KeyBloom recording or transmitting input.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, calm, and trustworthy. The play surface should feel vivid and celebratory without becoming frantic. The setup surface should feel native, plainspoken, and adult-controlled.

## Anti-references

- Rapid strobing, full-screen flashes, or stacked repeat effects that create localized flicker.
- Visually flat particles whose names promise more character than their motion delivers.
- Bright colors that wash out labels or disappear into a same-hued background.
- Decorative setup UI that obscures safety controls or does not preview the actual experience.
- Unpredictable key mappings that make the experience a light show instead of a discoverable system.

## Design Principles

1. Reward the arrival. A bloom should appear decisively, remain readable, and only then fade.
2. Make cause and effect learnable. A key keeps its color and its keyboard row keeps its effect family.
3. Keep celebration gentle. Smooth motion, bounded spawn rates, and calm-mode behavior prevent frantic or strobing output.
4. Show the real result before commitment. Palette, label, and motion settings preview the renderer itself.
5. Preserve adult trust. Safety, privacy, permission, and exit behavior stay explicit while play controls disappear during Baby Mode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

KeyBloom honors the macOS Reduce Motion preference, keeps a no-overshoot calm mode, and avoids flicker-inducing key-repeat behavior. Labels maintain strong figure/ground contrast across every palette. The setup screen uses native controls, visible selection states, keyboard focus behavior, and readable explanatory copy. The full-screen experience never depends on color alone: position, shape, motion, and optional labels reinforce each response.
